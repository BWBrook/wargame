# Barry context: *Battle of the Bulge* and *Unity of Command*

This file quarantines information supplied by Barry before independent research. It is context for the later fireside discussion, not external evidence that any feature succeeded generally.

Barry selected the original Shenandoah Studio *Battle of the Bulge* and the original 2x2 Games *Unity of Command* as a joint dossier. He described both as “a bit different” and worth studying for clever mechanics that might serve as nuclei of ideas, without implying that those mechanics should be copied.

He specifically recalled:

- the phased, asymmetric, alternating turns and single-area activation in *Battle of the Bulge*;
- the logistics imperatives and the wearing down of strong opposing forces through multiple ablation-style attacks in *Unity of Command*; and
- sustained personal engagement with both games: “They both certainly kept me hooked back in the day!”

Barry requested that the dossier cover only the two original games. Their sequels, continuations, DLC, ports, and later revisions may be used only to clarify the original object, document afterlife, or provide explicitly labelled retrospective evidence.

## Research handling

- The dossier assesses the nominated mechanisms because Barry identified them as objects of interest, but it does not treat his recollection as proof that they were universally successful.
- The two games are compared only within this requested pair. No corpus-wide synthesis or recommendation for the future fantasy game is made.
- In *Unity of Command*, only one formation can occupy a hex. Barry's “strong stack” is therefore interpreted as the remembered functional experience of a strong multi-step formation, not as a literal stack of counters.
- The 2012 iPad release of *Battle of the Bulge* is kept separate from the 2015 PC/Mac adaptation, whose store description explicitly advertises rewritten AI.
- The 2011 base *Unity of Command: Stalingrad Campaign* is kept separate from *Red Turn*, *Black Turn*, later free editor/update content, and *Unity of Command II*.
